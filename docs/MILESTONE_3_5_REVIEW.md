# Milestone 3.5 Review

## Review Date

2026-07-12.

## Repository Phase

Milestone 3.5: Art Direction Polish completed.

## Scope

This milestone refined the existing Milestone 3 guest-entry experience only:

1. Welcome.
2. Language selection.
3. Guest display name.
4. How to play.
5. Ready.
6. Quiz Coming Soon boundary remains unchanged.

No quiz engine, timer runtime, answer selection, scoring, leaderboard data, Supabase persistence, messages, admin actions, QA reset tools, deployment, QR generation, real Han facts, or real photos were added.

## Implemented Art Direction

- Reworked the public title into a reusable layered `TitleLockup` with chunky colored lettering, cream stroke, warm red outline, and blue offset depth.
- Added a reusable `HeroPhotoFrame` for the future approved Han image, with a stable portrait ratio, gingham tab, blue backing, neutral camera icon, localized placeholder copy, and image-load fallback.
- Replaced the flatter page background with a richer paper texture, stage/table base, and blue runner inspired by the physical party setup.
- Added reusable decorative motifs for balloons, gifts, and party hats.
- Refined `PaperPanel` and `Button` depth treatments so guest surfaces feel more like layered paper pieces.
- Redesigned onboarding progress as compact party-tag markers with accessible labels.
- Simplified the language switcher so it does not compete with the dedicated language screen.
- Reworked language choices, guest-name entry, how-to-play steps, and ready screen into warmer party compositions.

## Copy And Content

- Updated the welcome hero placeholder to say that Han's photo will go there.
- Updated the how-to-play lock instruction from tap-oriented wording to neutral submit-oriented wording.
- English and Vietnamese copy remains externalized in `content/en.json` and `content/vi.json`.
- No real Han memory, fact, quiz content, caption, or photo was invented.

## Visual Review Findings

- First visual pass showed the welcome CTA below the first mobile viewport; the mobile title and hero frame were tightened.
- The welcome CTA initially lived inside a continuously floating motion wrapper, causing Playwright to treat it as unstable; the motion now applies only to the portrait frame.
- The how-to-play screen initially pushed the CTA below the first mobile viewport; mobile spacing and card density were reduced.
- Desktop layouts now use wider scene composition rather than simply stretching a mobile column.
- Vietnamese welcome and placeholder text wraps cleanly without clipped diacritics in the captured states.

## Accessibility Findings

- The title lockup keeps accessible title text on the `h1` while decorative letter spans are `aria-hidden`.
- Decorative SVG motifs remain hidden from assistive technology.
- The progress indicator retains full accessible labels even where visible labels are compact.
- Name validation behavior and `aria-live` output from Milestone 3 are preserved.
- Reduced-motion capture passes for the Vietnamese welcome screen.
